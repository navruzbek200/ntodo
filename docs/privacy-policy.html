Ntodo Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026

Ntodo (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a simple to-do list app for Android. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how you can delete it. By using Ntodo you agree to this policy.

1. Data we collect

DataWhyStored where
UsernameTo create and identify your accountOur server
PasswordTo sign you inOur server (never stored on your phone)
Your tasks (title, done / not done)To show and sync your to-do listOur server
Sign-in tokenTo keep you signed inYour phone only; removed when you log out

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or advertising ID. The app contains no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

2. How we use your data

Only to provide the app’s features: creating your account, signing you in, and storing your tasks. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. Our server is hosted by Amazon Web Services (AWS), which stores the data on our behalf and may not use it for its own purposes. We may disclose data if required by law.

4. Retention

Your account and tasks are kept until you delete them or ask us to delete your account. Tasks you delete in the app are removed from our server immediately.

5. Deleting your account and data

You can request deletion of your Ntodo account and all associated data (username, password and all tasks) at any time:

  1. Send an email to navrozbekbektemirov7@gmail.com with the subject “Ntodo account deletion”.
  2. Include your Ntodo username. Send it from an address you can reply from, so we can confirm the request.

We delete the account and all its tasks within 30 days and reply to confirm. No data is kept after deletion, except where the law requires us to keep it. You can also delete individual tasks in the app at any time.

6. Security

Access to your tasks requires your password-protected account, and the sign-in token is removed from your device when you log out. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

7. Children

Ntodo is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.
